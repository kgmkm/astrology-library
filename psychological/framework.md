# Framework: How a Psychological Reading Is Built

This file states the reading method the companion skill (muse-horoscope) follows. Concepts are restated in our own words; sources are listed in SOURCES.md with their reuse status.

## The decomposition

A chart is read in four layers, in this order:

1. **Planet = a function.** What drive or faculty is speaking (see planets.md).
2. **Sign = the style of that function.** How the drive expresses itself (see signs.md).
3. **House = the arena.** Where in life the person meets it (see houses.md).
4. **Aspect = the conversation between functions.** Whether two drives support each other, clash, or need negotiation (see aspects.md).

A complete one-line reading combines the layers: function + style + arena, then the aspect story on top.

## The psychological turn (Rudhyar's legacy, restated)

- The chart is a map of the person's inner organisation, not a prediction machine. Planets symbolise human functions; houses show the circumstances through which denied or undeveloped functions are likely to be encountered (often first as other people or events — the projection idea from Jungian astrology).
- The Sun/Moon/Ascendant triad carries the basic portrait: the core self (Sun), the feeling self (Moon), and the presented self and temperament at the threshold of the world (Ascendant).
- Hard aspects are not "bad": in this tradition they mark the growth points, the places where energy is blocked until it is worked with. Soft aspects mark what flows without effort — and can therefore stay unnoticed.
- Cycles generalise: any two planets form a cycle with phases (the lunation cycle of Sun and Moon is the template). A transit reading is a snapshot of several such cycles touching the natal chart at once.

## House rules for readings in this project

- Light-touch entertainment. Readings describe tendencies and themes, never verdicts about a person's worth, health, money or fate.
- No cold reading: a reading may only weave in what that person has actually told their own Muse. Nothing is "divined" about facts the reader does not know.
- Hard configurations are phrased as work and texture ("in tension", "demands attention"), never as doom.
- Uranian material, when used, is one deep-dive point at most in a daily reading, because the hypothetical bodies move too slowly to change a daily picture by themselves.

# Aspects

Grounded in Sepharial, Ch. IV "The Astronomical Aspects" (1920) and Leo, *Astrology for All*, Ch. XVI appendix "Aspects". Both authors use the older good/evil terminology; this library renders it as harmonious / discordant, which is also the wording the companion skill uses for general readers.

| Aspect | Angle | Nature | Reading keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conjunction | 0° | variable | two planets united; takes on their own natures — favourable with Jupiter/Venus, difficult with Mars/Saturn (Sepharial); "variable" (Leo) |
| Sextile | 60° | harmonious | favourable, opportunity, good effects |
| Square | 90° | discordant | adverse, obstruction, friction that demands work |
| Trine | 120° | harmonious | favourable, harmonious, good effects, flow |
| Opposition | 180° | discordant | adverse, conflict, polarisation, awareness through tension |
| Semi-square | 45° | discordant (Sepharial); weak (Leo) | minor friction |
| Sesquiquadrate | 135° | discordant (Sepharial); weak (Leo) | minor friction |
| Parallel (declination) | — | as conjunction | same north/south declination; "acts as if in conjunction" (Sepharial only) |

Notes:
- Sepharial counts the semi-square and sesquiquadrate as fully discordant; Leo brackets 45° and 135° as comparatively weak and builds his working list on the five major aspects. The companion skill follows Leo's weighting: major aspects first, minor ones only when very tight.
- Leo lists a full polygon series (30°, 36°, 45°, 60°, 72°, 90°, 120°, 135°, 144°, 150°, 180°) and calls all but the five majors "comparatively weak, if not inconsiderable".
- A planet in hard aspect to another was called "afflicted" (Leo). The skill avoids that word on cards and says "in tension" instead.
- Orb practice in the companion skill: tighter orb = stronger reading. Natal readings foreground aspects under about 1°; daily transits are picked by tightness first.

# The Twelve Signs

Keywords distilled from public-domain sources (see Sources). Element, mode and ruler are facts; character keywords are short phrases from Alan Leo, Ch. XVI of *Astrology for All*, reworded only where his spelling is archaic. Sepharial's sign chapter (Ch. II) describes physical types rather than character, so character keywords here are Leo's.

Mode names: Leo uses Cardinal / Fixed / Mutable. Sepharial calls the same groups Movable / Fixed / Flexed.

Notes on disagreements between the sources:
- Aquarius: Sepharial (1920) gives Saturn as ruler of both Capricorn and Aquarius. Leo gives Uranus for Aquarius. This library lists the traditional ruler and the modern ruler side by side; readings in the companion skill use the modern ruler and may mention the traditional one.
- Group keywords (Leo): Fire = energy, force, life, passions, higher emotions. Earth = practical, business, solid, matter-of-fact. Air = intellectual, mental, artistic, professional. Water = receptive, personal feelings, sensitive, psychic. Cardinal = intellect, ambition, ideality. Fixed = will, power, purpose. Mutable = emotion, flexibility, restlessness.
- Sepharial adds older classifications: barren signs Aries, Leo, Capricorn; fruitful Taurus, Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces; double-bodied Gemini, Sagittarius, Pisces. These are historical colour, not used by the companion skill's readings.

# Planets as Psychological Functions

Framework: in psychological astrology a planet is read as a *function* of the person — a drive or faculty — not as a transmitter of physical influence. This framing follows Dane Rudhyar (planets as "symbols of human functions", summarised in Glenn Perry's history of psychological astrology; see SOURCES.md — link-only sources, concepts restated here in our own words).

Role names are Alan Leo's, from the chapter titles of *The Art of Synthesis* (1912) and *How to Judge a Nativity* (1904), both public domain. Leo's own summary in *Astrology for All*: the Sun is the Individuality, the Moon the Personality. One-line meanings below are this library's plain-language restatements, written for general readers.

| Planet | Leo's role name | Plain meaning | Keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | the Life-Giver | the core of the person; "who I want to be"; the source of vitality | centre, purpose, vitality |
| Moon | the Mother | feelings and the need for safety; moods, habits, what feels like home | feelings, safety, habit |
| Mercury | the Thinker | how the mind works and communicates; curiosity and connection | thought, words, curiosity |
| Venus | the Unifier | what we love and find beautiful; the urge to join and harmonise | love, beauty, harmony |
| Mars | the Energiser | drive and assertion; how we go after what we want, and how anger moves | action, desire, spark |
| Jupiter | the Uplifter | growth and widening horizons; faith, learning, generosity | growth, optimism, learning |
| Saturn | the Subduer | limits, duty and time; where we feel tested, and where slow mastery is built | challenge, endurance, duty |
| Uranus | the Awakener | change, originality and sudden insight; breaking the pattern | change, originality, freedom |
| Neptune | the Mystic | dreams, imagination and dissolving boundaries; longing and compassion | dreams, imagination, empathy |
| Pluto | — (undiscovered in Leo's lifetime) | deep transformation; what we cannot let go of, and where we are remade | depth, transformation, attachment |

Sepharial's significators (Ch. I and Ch. IV of his 1920 manual, public domain) agree in outline and add period colour: Sun — magnanimous, noble, loyal, honours, the father; Moon — adaptable, variable, change, the mother; Mercury — active, versatile, commerce of mind or market, knowledge; Venus — love affairs, domestic relations, pleasures; Mars — enterprises, strifes; Jupiter — increase, the most benefic; Saturn — legacies, inheritance, aged persons; Uranus — civic and governmental bodies; Neptune — voyages, psychic experiences.

Note: Pluto has no public-domain role name in this library's sources. The line above is a modern completion in the same style, and it is marked as such wherever the companion skill uses it.

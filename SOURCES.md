# Source Registry

Every entry in this library traces to a source listed here, with a reuse verdict decided from the source's own pages (checked 2026-11-08). This is a working registry, not legal advice.

Verdicts:
- **include** — may be quoted or summarised into this repository, under the stated conditions.
- **summary-only** — facts, methods and ideas may be restated in our own words; the text itself is never copied.
- **link-only** — cite and link; do not extract content.
- **excluded** — do not use; reason stated.

## Public domain — include (edition limits apply)
- Sepharial, *Astrology: How to Make and Read Your Own Horoscope* (1920), Project Gutenberg #46963. Planets / signs / houses / aspects chapters plus planets-in-houses and character reading. Condition: the 1920 text only. In practice we summarise into plain modern language.
- Alan Leo, *How to Judge a Nativity* (1904), *The Art of Synthesis* (1912), *Astrology for All* (1904; scanned 1931 sixth-edition re-issue consulted). Planet role names, sign keywords, Sun = Individuality / Moon = Personality. Condition: early editions only; modern reprints that assert fresh copyright are not used.
- Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos*, Ashmand translation, Project Gutenberg #70850. Historical grounding for planet natures and sign classification. Not psychological astrology itself.
- William Lilly, *Christian Astrology* (1647/1659). Classical house meanings. Condition: texts derived from the original editions only; a 2003 modern transcription circulating on archive.org is not used.
- Alan Leo, *The Progressed Horoscope* (1906, L. N. Fowler edition). Transit delineation: a transits section, each planet's transit through the twelve houses, and the general effect of transits. Condition: the 1906 edition only (later printings circulate, one dated 1930), and OCR quality is poor, so it is used as an approach source rather than a quotable text.

## CC BY-SA — include only in a segregated section
- English Wikipedia: "Planets in astrology", "Astrological sign", "House (astrology)", "Astrological aspect", "Hamburg School of Astrology". Modern framing sentences and tables. Condition: attribution + share-alike. If used at all, Wikipedia-derived files live only under a clearly marked CC BY-SA section; otherwise these articles are treated as summary-only. (Current library files restate facts from them without copying text.)
- Wikidata structured data is CC0 (verified on Wikidata's licensing page) and may be used freely for fact tables (names, rulers, date ranges, multilingual labels). Cite anyway.

## Summary-only (no licence statement found; default rights reserved)
- Arlene Kramer, "The Eight TransNeptunian 'Planets' of the Uranian System" (planetwaves.net) — main source behind uranian/tnp8.md summaries.
- Bruce Scofield, "An Outline of the Uranian System" (Kepler College library PDF) — technique outline behind uranian/midpoints.md.
- Moira Astro, Lexicon: "Midpoint" — technique cross-check.
- Augurine, Learn guides (Planetary Pictures; Aries Point) — method and orb practice. One worked example there quotes Ebertin's COSI; that quoted text is not carried over.
- Uranian Astrology School, *Beginners Guide to Uranian Astrology* — midpoint definition cross-check.
- WDIC (Japanese), "Uranian astrology" — names, periods, one-word symbols.

## Link-only
- Cafe Astrology — its terms assert exclusive copyright over site content. Useful as reading, never as a text source.
- astro.com / Astrodienst (including Liz Greene's psychological horoscope texts) — terms prohibit systematic extraction and redistribution.
- Khaldea / Rudhyar Archival Project — published by the author's estate's permission, with an explicit no-reproduction notice on the site. Concepts may be restated; text is linked, not copied.
- Hiroki Niizato's astrology notes (Japanese) — reference and cross-checking only.

## Excluded (important)
- **Sabian Symbols — excluded for now.** A public-domain claim could not be verified: 1925 is the year of creation, but the first book publication of the set was 1953 and Marc Edmund Jones died in 1980, so copyright plausibly persists in the US. Datasets that merely assert public-domain status are not bundled. Revisit only with a proper legal check.
- Ebertin, *The Combination of Stellar Influences*, and the Witte *Regelwerk* (Rules for Planetary Pictures) — no open edition exists. Paid / rights-reserved; this library reads combinations by keyword synthesis instead of pretending to replace the dictionaries.
- Kerykeion — AGPL-3.0. The companion skill's calculation layer is openephem (MIT) by design decision, so Kerykeion is not used.
- Astrolog (GPL-2.0) — its own texts are usable under GPL terms, but its bundled Swiss Ephemeris component is under separate terms. Reference only, for now.
- GitHub dataset "vedic_karakas" — its MIT licence covers the JSON structure only; the compilation's rights remain with its original author. Schema reference only (and it is Vedic, not Western).
- Final sweep additions (2026-11-08): no GitHub or Hugging Face dataset with original Western interpretation prose under a permissive licence was found. Specifically: the Hugging Face dataset "ZodiacViews/zodiac-horoscope-daily" has no licence field, gated access and unstated provenance — excluded. "RoxyAPI/kundli-api" is a sample client for a paid API; no interpretation text is in the repo — excluded as a dataset. "Libra Rising: Planets in the signs (144 positions)" is complete but a normal copyrighted website — link-only at best. "Dajeki/tarot-api" has no licence file at all — link-only. The stellium project's astrology guide is AGPL-3.0 — facts may be paraphrased, text is not reused. A third-party skill collection ("openclaw-master-skills", MIT) mirrors a sun-sign trait skill of unverified originality — summary-only with caution, not an interpretation corpus. TropicalAstro's moon-phase page (no licence statement) is cited for Rudhyar's phase names and degree boundaries only, which are facts.

# Contributing

The one rule that matters here: **no source, no entry.**

1. Before adding content, add the source to SOURCES.md with a reuse verdict (include / summary-only / link-only / excluded) and the evidence for it — a licence statement on the source's own page, a publication year and author's death year for public-domain claims, or the terms page for exclusions.
2. Public-domain material may be summarised freely; name the edition. Modern reprints sometimes assert new rights over old texts — check, and use early editions.
3. Summary-only material: restate facts, methods and ideas in your own words. Never paste passages, and never launder a copyrighted dictionary through a third site that quotes it.
4. If a rights claim cannot be verified (an assertion without evidence), the verdict is excluded until it can. The Sabian Symbols entry in SOURCES.md is the standing example.
5. Keep the language plain. The readers are people interested in astrology, not specialists; only the word "horoscope" itself is allowed to be technical without explanation.
6. Disagreements between sources are recorded, not smoothed over (see the Aquarius ruler note in psychological/signs.md).

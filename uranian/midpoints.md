# Midpoints and Planetary Pictures

Technique reference, written in this library's own words. Formulas and procedures are facts and methods. The outlines used to cross-check them (Scofield's Kepler College outline, the Moira Astro lexicon entry, Augurine's learn guides) carry no open licence, so nothing is copied from them; see SOURCES.md. The combination dictionaries (Ebertin's *Combination of Stellar Influences*; the Witte *Regelwerk*) have no open edition and are not reproduced here — readings use keyword synthesis.

## Midpoint (half-sum)

- The midpoint of two bodies A and B is the average of their longitudes: (A + B) / 2, worked in 0–360°. There are two midpoints, near and far.
- When a third body C sits on the A/B midpoint, written A/B = C, the themes of A and B are read as working together at C.
- A midpoint is an axis, not just a point: the opposite degree belongs to the same axis. On a 90° dial the four axis points coincide, which is why midpoint work and the dial belong together.

## Folding onto the 90° dial

- Reduce longitude modulo 90°. Cardinal, fixed and mutable signs stack in order.
- On the dial, conjunctions and the hard series (0° / 45° / 90° / 135° / 180°) land on the same axis, so a whole midpoint structure becomes visible at a glance.

## Personal points (where reading starts)

- MC, Ascendant, Sun, Moon, the Moon's Node, and the Aries Point (the 0° Aries axis; read as the person's contact with the world at large).
- In a natal reading, midpoints and bodies sitting on these points are picked up first.

## Planetary pictures

- A planetary picture is the whole set of bodies and midpoints sharing one axis, written as an equation chain, e.g. A/B = C/D.
- Reading by synthesis: blend the left pair's themes; the right side shows where or how that blend manifests.
- Orb practice: midpoint contacts are taken tight, roughly 1–1.5° at most. The companion skill sorts by tightness and prefers the tightest.

## How the companion skill uses this

- Natal detail reading: midpoints on personal points are prime material (a worked example from development: Sun/Ascendant = Cupido at 0.00° orb).
- Daily reading: at most one Uranian deep-dive point, and only when a fast transiting body touches a natal midpoint axis or TNP. The TNPs move about a degree a year or less, so they cannot carry a daily reading alone.

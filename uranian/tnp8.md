# The Eight Trans-Neptunian Points (Uranian / Hamburg School)

The eight hypothetical points of the Hamburg School: four proposed by Alfred Witte (Cupido, Hades, Zeus, Kronos) and four added by Friedrich Sieggrün in 1927 (Apollon, Admetos, Vulcanus, Poseidon). In this project they are called "German hypothetical bodies" on Japanese-language cards; in English materials the standard name is Trans-Neptunian Points (TNPs). Names stay in English in all languages, by design.

Meanings below are this library's own summaries, distilled mainly from Arlene Kramer's delineations (see SOURCES.md — summary-only source; no text is reproduced). Periods differ slightly between sources and are given as approximate; they are for orientation only, never for calculation.

| Point | Keywords | Plain meaning | Approx. period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cupido | gathering, family and groups, art | the container where people come together; Venus's "I like" widened into "our place". A super-Venus: where Venus means beauty, Cupido means art; where Venus means love, Cupido means marriage and belonging | ~262 years (fastest of the eight) |
| Hades | the past, buried things, depth | old, discarded and hidden things coming to light; antiquity and depth, not only decay | ~361 years |
| Zeus | directed energy, creative drive, machines | energy aimed at a target; planned making and moving | ~456 years |
| Kronos | authority, the recognised expert, highest standard | standing as "the one who knows"; quality and public standing | ~522 years |
| Apollon | expansion, increase, learning and trade | a super-Jupiter: knowledge and circles widening sideways | ~576 years |
| Admetos | immobility, narrowing, deep specialisation | stopping at one point and digging; standstill on the surface, expertise underneath | ~617–624 years |
| Vulcanus | immense power, strength, pressure | power itself, out of scale; energy that cannot be contained | ~663 years |
| Poseidon | truth, spirit, illumination | knowing "how it really is"; clarity rather than fog — a super-Neptune without the mist | ~720–740 years |

How combinations are read: a pairing is read as "the themes of B working in the territory of A". Example from Kramer: Sun + Cupido = the person as artist, spouse, or member of a group; the body able to express an art. The full combination dictionary lives in Witte's *Rules for Planetary Pictures*, which is paid and untranslated; this library deliberately does not reproduce it and reads by keyword synthesis instead (see midpoints.md).

History in one line: Reinhold Ebertin, a student of the Hamburg methods, dropped the hypothetical points while keeping midpoints and the dial, and renamed the result Cosmobiology (1940). Uranian astrology keeps the points; Cosmobiology does not.
